## Introduction

Hi friend(s). If youre reading this, you found my website! I have had many, many iterations of a personal website (and this is likely the first you've ever seen) BUT this one, hopefully proven by the fact I am actually sharing it - is here to stay. It is powered by HUGO, hosted on GitHub pages with a custom domain. No AI involved here.

## What to expect

Here you will find my haiku poetry, [komorebi photography](https://japaneserituals.com/komorebi/),and blog posts about general musings, thoughts, and experiences. This can be about zen practice, games, or tech I enjoy. There will be some haibun, and prose / stories as well but I dont want to promise too much yet. I find my main problems with websites is biting off so much more than I can chew and then abandoning all together. So for now: haiku / komorebi / blog. 

## Why

I am no longer an active part of mainline social media. I have deleted my profiles on facebook, instagram, reddit, tiktok, etc. Anything with a feed I tend to stay away from. Its just too addicting, and my brain has a hard time resisting its pull. You will find links to my letterboxd and goodreads here. I mention it because it is technically social media. I just cannot bring myself to manually track every piece of media I consume on my website. Too much work. If you want to keep up with all that I will have those links readily available at the bottom of the page. 

I do hope the haiku and photography you find here will bring a sort of peace to you, or a quiet moment.
